Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

In short
  • We collect what the app needs to run: your account, your training records, and your subscription status.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Analytics never include your weights, reps, or notes.
  • You can delete your account and its training data at any time.

This policy explains how the OneSet iOS app ("OneSet", "we", "us") handles your personal data. OneSet is run by Dimitar Radulov, an individual developer based in the European Union, who is the data controller for the data described here.

What we collect

Account information

When you sign in with Apple, Google, or an email code, we receive your email address, your name if the provider shares it, and an account identifier. If you use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive only the relay address Apple gives us.

Training data

The information you enter while using the app: your units and training frequency, the programs you select and your progress through them, and your workout sessions, including exercises, weights, reps, completion times, substitutions, and any notes you write.

Subscription information

If you start a trial or subscribe, Apple processes the payment. We receive your subscription status, the plan, renewal and expiry dates, and transaction identifiers. We never receive your payment card or billing details.

Usage analytics

We record a small set of pseudonymous events to understand whether the app works well: completing onboarding, the program chosen, trial conversion, workout start and finish, and sync failures. These events never contain your weights, reps, notes, email, or name. We don't use session recording or replay.

Technical data

When the app talks to our servers, our hosting provider processes your IP address, request times, and basic device and app version information to deliver the service, prevent abuse, and diagnose errors. Error logs contain codes and identifiers, not your training data.

Data stored on your device

OneSet keeps a copy of your training data, the program catalog, and any downloaded exercise videos on your device so you can train offline. Signing out removes the account's local data from the device.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal data.
  • We don't show advertising or share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, location, or Apple Health data.

How long we keep it

  • Account and training data: for as long as your account exists. Deleting a workout session removes it permanently.
  • After you delete your account: we erase your account and training data from our live systems. Encrypted backups age out on a rolling schedule and are not used to restore deleted accounts.
  • Subscription records: as long as needed to provide access and meet legal and accounting duties.
  • Analytics and server logs: kept only as long as they're useful for diagnosing problems.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app under Settings > Account, or by emailing us at the address below. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription. To stop being charged, cancel it in your device's Settings under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy
  • correct inaccurate data
  • delete your data
  • receive your data in a portable format
  • object to or restrict processing based on our legitimate interests
  • complain to your local data protection authority

To use any of these rights, email us. We'll reply within one month.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your training data can be read only through your signed-in account, exercise videos are served only to authorized users, and backups are kept in private storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and limit who can access it.

Children

OneSet is not intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
